# Google Play Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Final app name and business developer identity
- [ ] Business-owned application ID and signing key
- [ ] App icon, feature graphic, and screenshots
- [ ] Privacy policy URL and support URL
- [ ] Data Safety declarations based on actual collection
- [ ] Content rating questionnaire
- [ ] Test account and staging/test payment instructions
- [ ] Release build and device testing
- [ ] Crash reporting and analytics disclosure
- [ ] Review notes and production support process

Do not submit until legal copy, privacy disclosures, developer ownership, and payment behavior are final.
