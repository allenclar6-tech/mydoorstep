# Apple App Store Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Final app name and business developer identity
- [ ] Bundle ID, signing certificates, and provisioning profiles
- [ ] App icon and required device screenshots
- [ ] Privacy policy URL and support URL
- [ ] App Privacy nutrition labels based on actual data flows
- [ ] Age rating and content declarations
- [ ] TestFlight build and test account
- [ ] Payment, location, notification, and camera permission explanations
- [ ] App Review notes and demo workflow
- [ ] Release and rollback plan

Do not submit until business ownership, legal copy, provider integrations, and privacy disclosures are approved.
